# ChillGo — Legal Pages (Sections 5.5 & 8)

**These are drafts, not legal advice.** Start from Shopify's built-in policy
generator (Settings > Policies), then paste/merge these adjustments. Anything in
`{{ }}` is a real value only the operator can supply — fill it before publishing.

Placeholders you must fill everywhere they appear:
- `{{legal_name}}` — the name on the c/o address service
- `{{c_o_address}}` — the anonymous c/o postal address (same pattern as your
  other ventures' Impressum)
- `{{contact_email}}` — a support email you actually monitor (async, Section 7)
- `{{store_url}}` — chillgo.com (or galejet.com)
- `{{warehouse_region}}` / `{{delivery_window}}` — the **real** figures for the
  warehouse the sourcing script picked (Section 4). Never generic filler.

Hard requirements baked into these drafts:
- **EU Widerrufsrecht (14-day withdrawal)** is present and cannot be watered down
  for physical goods (Section 5.5).
- **No fake countdown timers or fabricated scarcity** — UWG risk (Section 5.5/8).
- Shipping language matches the actual warehouse, not a default "2–5 days".

Get these reviewed by someone qualified before going live — a store selling
lithium-battery goods into the EU has real compliance exposure.
