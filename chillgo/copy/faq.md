### How long does the battery last?
Runtime depends on the speed. You get the longest run on low and the shortest on
turbo — the exact tested range is in the spec box above. In plain terms: a full
charge comfortably covers a commute, a walk, or an afternoon out. _(Fill the
real `{{runtime_low}}`/`{{runtime_turbo}}` from the tested sample — no rounding
up.)_

### How long does it take to charge?
About {{charge_time}} from empty over USB-C. Top it up from any phone charger,
laptop, or power bank — no special adapter, no proprietary cable.

### How loud is it on the highest setting?
Turbo has a noticeable whoosh — it's moving real air, so it's not silent. Low and
medium are quiet enough for a desk or the office. It's a fan, not a whisper, and
we won't pretend otherwise.

### How long will shipping actually take?
Orders ship from our local warehouse, so most arrive within the delivery window
shown at checkout for your country. We keep this figure matched to the **real**
warehouse — no fake "2-day" promises. _(Set the actual window from the warehouse
the sourcing script picked — if it ever ships from China, say 10–20 days, don't
default to "2–5 days.")_

### What's your return policy?
30-day returns. EU customers keep the full 14-day right of withdrawal
(Widerrufsrecht). If it's not for you, start a return straight from your order
confirmation email — most requests are fully self-service, no call needed.
