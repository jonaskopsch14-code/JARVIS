## Track from day 1 (Section 11), even at €0 ad spend
- Bio-link click-through rate
- Add-to-cart rate
- Conversion rate
- Average order value (watch the bundle lift it)
- Refund rate
- **Video watch-time + completion rate** ← the signal that picks the week-2 hook

## Week-1 success bar (realistic — Section 11)
Store live · 5–7 videos posted · pixel data flowing · a directional read on which
hook resonates. **A handful of organic sales in week 1 is a good outcome** — full
profitability is a scaling-phase goal, not launch week.

## Week 2
Take the hook with the best watch-time/completion, shoot 3–4 fresh variations of
it, and (optionally) put €1–5/day for 3–5 days behind the single best organic
video via TikTok's in-app **Promote** — not full Ads Manager (Section 12 budget).
