# ChillGo — TikTok / Reels Scripts (Section 10)

**Format for every video:** product footage + text overlay + trending audio.
**No face, no voice, no talking-head.** 7–15 seconds. End on a CTA text card
("Link in bio"). Pick the trending audio **fresh at posting time** — audio
trends shift daily and can't be pre-selected.

Five hook templates, each with 3 variations you can rotate. Shoot the sample
unit (Section 12) for original B-roll — self-filmed footage out-performs generic
supplier clips for organic reach.

## Shooting checklist (self-filmed B-roll)
- Natural light, clean background, phone stabilised.
- Always capture: fan-in-hand, pull-from-bag, ribbon/paper flutter (get a real
  ribbon or receipt tape), close-up of the speed button + battery display.
- Shoot vertical 9:16, 1080p, 60fps for slow-mo headroom.
- Keep hooks in the **first 1.5 seconds** — that's what decides watch-time.
- No fabricated claims on overlays (no "lasts 24h" unless the sample proves it).
